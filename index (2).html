01 / WHY DIGITAL HOPE

Technology shouldn't be a privilege.

In many rural classrooms, students learn about computers without getting the chance to use one. At the same time, businesses and individuals often replace devices that still have years of useful life.

Digital Hope brings these two realities together. We help donors pledge usable equipment and connect it with schools that have shared their requirements.
